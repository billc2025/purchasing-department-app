# Purchasing Hub user guide

## Sign in and language

Purchasing Hub is invitation-only. Use the invitation email and sign in at the organization’s approved URL. Choose **English** or **Español** in the header; the preference follows your account.

## Place an order

1. Select **New order**.
2. Choose who the order is for, its category, delivery location, and required-by date.
3. Choose who bears the cost: **Client billed** or **Internal cost**. Client-billed orders require the client reference.
4. Enter the budget for the entire order.
5. Add each item with quantity, unit, specification, and estimated item cost.
6. Add optional reference images. JPEG, PNG, and WebP files are accepted up to 8 MB.
7. Review the order summary, save the draft, and submit when ready.

The lead-time message explains whether the required-by date is compliant. Late requests enter an exception decision instead of silently bypassing policy.

## Follow an order

**My orders** shows your requests. Open an order to see who requested it, item quantities, estimated and actual costs, vendor/receipt activity, comments, and status. The bell in the header shows unread alerts and links directly to records you are authorized to view.

If purchasing requests information, reply in the shared comments. Internal notes are not visible to requesters.

## Receipt and problems

When all expected items are recorded as received, the requester confirms that the order is correct. Report missing, incorrect, damaged, or incomplete items with details. The order remains active until the issue is resolved.

## Changes and cancellation

An unassigned order may be directly editable before the configured cutoff. Later material changes require a recorded request and decision. Cancellation before purchasing is simple; after assignment or purchase it follows a formal workflow and may require financial outcomes for each purchased item.

## Purchasing and reception

Purchasing agents claim work from the purchasing bucket, review items individually, request information, record vendors and separate receipts, and dispatch finalized items. Reception records partial or complete delivery only in statuses permitted by configuration.

## Getting help

If access is awaiting provisioning, inactive, or an order appears stale, do not create a duplicate immediately. Refresh once, note the order number and message, and contact an administrator.

## Administrator invitation summary

Administrators invite users through Clerk. Once an invitation is accepted, the new account appears under **Settings → Users and roles** as a Requester. An authorized administrator can then select the approved visible role, enter a reason, and save the audited change.
